Home › Premier League › Liverpool and Chelsea target Nottingham Forest defender Murillo

Liverpool and Chelsea are keeping tabs on Nottingham Forest defender Murillo ahead of a potential move in 2027, but Forest have no intention of allowing the Brazil international to leave during the January transfer window.

The 24-year-old has established himself as one of Forest’s key players since arriving at the City Ground and continues to attract interest from several leading clubs. Liverpool and Chelsea have now joined the group monitoring his situation, with Manchester United also understood to have previously identified him as a target.

Liverpool’s interest comes as the club considers its long-term options at centre-back, while Chelsea are also looking to strengthen their squad. However, neither club is expected to be able to test Forest’s resolve in January.

Football Insider reports that Forest are not under financial pressure to sell Murillo and have placed a substantial valuation on the defender. His position is further protected by a contract running until 2030, which he signed in May.

Liverpool and Chelsea target Nottingham Forest defender Murillo

Forest have already demonstrated their determination to retain their leading players, particularly after Elliot Anderson’s record-breaking move to Manchester City. That stance is expected to continue into the winter window.

The possibility of a summer transfer could therefore be more significant. Clubs are expected to continue monitoring Murillo, with Liverpool and Chelsea potentially joined by further suitors if Forest eventually become willing to negotiate.

Murillo’s performances have made him one of the most sought-after defenders outside the Premier League’s established elite, while his long-term contract gives Forest considerable leverage.

For now, however, the message from the City Ground is clear: Murillo is not available in January, leaving interested clubs to wait until the summer before deciding whether to make a serious approach.
