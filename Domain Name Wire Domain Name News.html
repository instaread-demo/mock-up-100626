Craig Schwartz retiring, search begins for fTLD’s next president

Andrew Allemann Leave a Comment October 2, 2026

Company behind .bank and .insurance is looking for a new leader.

Listen to audio version of this article

A longtime fixture in the domain name business is retiring, opening up an interesting job opportunity.

Craig Schwartz, president of fTLD, announced that he is retiring in Q2 2027. He’s led the company since 2011, after spending five years at ICANN.

fTLD manages the highly controlled .bank and .insurance top level domain names. Both domains are limited to qualified institutions in their respective industries.

There are about 2,500 .bank domains in the zone and 150 or so .insurance domains. Retail pricing is around $1,000 per year.

fTLD did not apply for any domains in the 2026 expansion round.

Headhunting firm Perrett Laver is managing the search for Schwartz’s replacement.

The role is remote, with about 20% spent traveling, particularly to Washington, D.C.

Total compensation is in the $375k-$425k range.
