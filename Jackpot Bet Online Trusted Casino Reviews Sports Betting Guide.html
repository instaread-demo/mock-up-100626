Buried By Night Slot Review

Buried By Night is an upcoming Wild West slot from Hacksaw Gaming that puts a darker spin on the developer’s familiar gunslinger formula. Instead of building the game around a conventional set of expanding reels or vertical multiplier wilds, Buried By Night introduces DuelRows—horizontal wild sections capable of carrying multipliers and transforming an otherwise ordinary line win into something substantially larger.

The slot is played on a 5-reel, 4-row grid with 14 fixed paylines. Its published headline RTP reaches 96.32%, while the maximum win is capped at 15,000x the stake. Multiple current game databases list the official release for 10 November 2026, meaning this review is based on pre-release game information, demos and independently published specifications available ahead of the full public launch.

That distinction matters. Buried By Night has already appeared on several review and demo platforms, but at the time of writing it has not yet reached its scheduled general release date. Players should therefore check the final in-game information screen when it becomes available at their chosen licensed casino, particularly because RTP configurations and certain features can vary between markets.

From the mechanics currently documented, however, Buried By Night looks considerably more interesting than its familiar cowboy theme initially suggests.

At its core, Buried By Night remains easy to understand. Wins are formed using matching symbols across the game’s fixed paylines, beginning from the left side of the grid.

The conventional payline structure is important because this is not a cluster-pays or ways-to-win slot. The excitement instead comes from what happens when a VS symbol interacts with an existing winning combination.

According to current published game information, VS symbols can appear on reels 2, 3 or 4. When the right conditions are met, the symbol expands horizontally to create a DuelRow.

The position of the VS symbol determines how much of the grid it covers:

  • A VS symbol on reel 2 can cover reels 1 to 3.
  • A VS symbol on reel 3 can expand across all five reels.
  • A VS symbol on reel 4 can cover reels 3 to 5.

These expanded sections behave as wilds and introduce one of the slot’s defining mechanics: a duel between two multiplier values. The surviving multiplier is attached to the DuelRow and can enhance qualifying wins involving that row. Multiple participating DuelRows can become especially important because their multipliers can be combined.

That gives Buried By Night a straightforward basic game with a much more volatile second layer sitting underneath it.

Theme, Graphics and Presentation

Hacksaw Gaming is hardly new to the Western genre. The developer has previously explored violent frontier settings, gunslingers and outlaw imagery in games such as Wanted Dead or a Wild and Duel at Dawn.

Buried By Night returns to that territory with a deliberately grim atmosphere.

The setting uses the familiar language of a Western showdown: dusty landscapes, an ominous horizon, guns, skulls, sheriff imagery and a lone gunslinger preparing for confrontation. Current previews describe an orange-red or sunset-like backdrop, while the artwork has a painted, cinematic quality rather than the brighter cartoon styling found in more casual Western slots.

The symbol selection follows the same theme. Card-suit symbols occupy the lower end of the paytable, while more valuable symbols include objects such as wagon wheels, skulls, cowboy hats, pistols and sheriff badges.

There is nothing revolutionary about those symbols on their own. The identity of Buried By Night comes primarily from the VS symbols and the duels they create.

This is an important distinction because Wild West slots are hardly rare. Another generic collection of cowboy symbols would struggle to stand out. The horizontal DuelRows give Buried By Night a recognisable mechanical identity even if its visual theme feels familiar.

How DuelRows Work

DuelRows are the feature that deserves the most attention in any Buried By Night slot review.

When a VS symbol appears in the necessary position and forms part of the required winning setup, it can stretch horizontally across part or all of the grid. Once expanded, the DuelRow functions as a wild area.

A duel then determines a multiplier.

Current game information lists potential DuelRow multipliers ranging from 2x to 100x. When the resulting DuelRow participates in a win, its multiplier is applied to the relevant payout. If more than one DuelRow contributes to the same combination, the applicable multipliers can be added together.

This additive approach is where the game’s higher potential starts to become clear.

For example, imagine two DuelRows become involved in a qualifying win. If one contributes a 10x multiplier and another contributes 20x, the combined effect can become considerably more valuable than a standard wild substitution.

It is worth stressing that multipliers do not change the underlying probability of winning a spin. They simply change the value of qualifying outcomes when the appropriate mechanics activate.

That distinction becomes particularly important in a high-volatility game. A large theoretical multiplier does not mean those outcomes will occur frequently.

Trigger Warning Free Spins

The first major bonus mode is called Trigger Warning.

Published information indicates that landing three FS Scatter symbols during the base game triggers 10 free spins. During this feature, VS symbols appear more frequently, increasing the opportunities to activate DuelRows.

The feature can also be extended.

Current rules indicate that landing additional FS Scatters during the round can award extra free spins. That means a session that begins with 10 spins does not necessarily have to end after the original allocation.

Trigger Warning appears to be the most accessible interpretation of the core Buried By Night mechanic. It does not dramatically reinvent the game. Instead, it increases the frequency of the symbols responsible for its most important feature.

That can be effective because the DuelRows already provide enough complexity. Rather than adding an entirely separate bonus system, Trigger Warning puts more emphasis on the mechanic players have already learned in the base game.

Shoot to Kill Bonus

Shoot to Kill is the more elaborate free-spins feature.

Current game information states that landing four FS Scatter symbols triggers 8 free spins. What makes this mode different is the introduction of a DuelSpin Bar.

FS Scatter symbols collected during the bonus advance the bar. Progressing through its tiers can award DuelSpins, additional free spins and a growing number of Nudges.

The currently reported structure is:

  • Tier 1: at least 1 VS symbol, 3 Nudges and 2 additional free spins
  • Tier 2: at least 2 VS symbols, 4 Nudges and 2 additional free spins
  • Tier 3: at least 3 VS symbols, 5 Nudges and 2 additional free spins
  • Tier 4: at least 4 VS symbols, 6 Nudges and 2 additional free spins

A DuelSpin is awarded when the appropriate progress level is reached, giving the player a spin containing guaranteed VS activity based on the unlocked tier.

This feature appears to be where Buried By Night’s mechanics reach their highest intensity.

Instead of simply waiting for another VS symbol, players are effectively progressing toward increasingly powerful DuelSpin setups.

Nudges Explained

The Nudge mechanic works alongside DuelSpins in the Shoot to Kill feature.

After the initial result, the reels can move down simultaneously by one position. Any new qualifying wins created after the movement are evaluated before another Nudge takes place.

Why does this matter?

Because an active DuelRow may continue contributing as the symbols around it change. This gives a single DuelSpin the possibility of producing several evaluated positions rather than one static outcome.

A strong combination of DuelRows, multipliers and multiple Nudges is therefore one of the situations in which the game’s high maximum potential begins to make mathematical sense.

It also reinforces the high-variance character of the slot. Many ordinary spins may be comparatively uneventful, while a much smaller number of feature sequences can contain substantially more activity.

Six Feet Under and the Hot 100 Scatters

Buried By Night includes another unusual bonus called Six Feet Under.

Current published rules state that players trigger it by landing three Hot 100 Scatter symbols.

Unlike a conventional free-spins round offering a fixed number of spins, Six Feet Under initially awards a single spin and uses repeated Nudges. The sequence continues until 100 winning outcomes have been counted.

It is an interesting concept because it changes the objective from “how many spins are left?” to “how long can this evolving sequence continue before its completion condition is reached?”

The wording can sound more generous than it necessarily is, however. A feature guaranteeing a particular number of counted wins should not be confused with guaranteeing a particular monetary return. The size of those wins still matters.

As always with slots, the headline mechanic should be judged alongside the game’s volatility and overall mathematical model rather than in isolation.

Buried By Night RTP

The highest widely reported Buried By Night RTP is 96.32%.

However, multiple RTP configurations have been reported for the game. Current databases list versions including 94.34%, 92.26% and, on some platforms, 86.35%, in addition to the 96.32% configuration.

This is one of the most important details for players to check before playing.

RTP, or Return to Player, represents the theoretical percentage of stakes that a game is designed to return over a very large statistical sample. It does not mean that someone betting £100 should expect to receive £96.32 back during an individual session.

Short-term outcomes can be dramatically different, particularly with a volatile slot.

An RTP of 96.32% corresponds to a theoretical house edge of 3.68% before considering any other casino-specific conditions. If a casino operates a lower-RTP version of the game, the theoretical house edge is correspondingly higher.

For that reason, checking the information or help menu inside the actual game is more reliable than assuming every casino hosts the same RTP configuration.

Buried By Night Volatility and Hit Frequency

Buried By Night is generally classified as a high-volatility or medium-high-volatility slot depending on the review source. Its reported hit frequency is approximately 29% to 29.2%.

Hit frequency describes how often a spin produces some form of winning result across the game’s long-term mathematical model. It does not indicate whether that win will exceed the original stake.

This distinction is particularly relevant in modern high-volatility slots.

A game can technically produce a “win” while still returning less than the amount wagered on that spin. Equally, the average hit rate says nothing about how wins will be distributed during an individual session.

Buried By Night’s 15,000x ceiling, multiplier-based DuelRows and feature-heavy design all point toward a game in which a meaningful part of the theoretical return can be concentrated in less common outcomes.

Players who prefer frequent, relatively predictable rewards may therefore find the game uneven.

Those who specifically enjoy high-variance mechanics will probably find the structure more familiar.

Maximum Win: 15,000x the Stake

The Buried By Night max win is 15,000x the total stake. This figure is consistently reported by the major current game-information sources covering the release.

At a hypothetical £1 stake, that ceiling corresponds to £15,000 before considering operator-specific restrictions or regulatory limits.

Naturally, the maximum-win figure is a ceiling, not an expected result.

This is one of the most common areas where slot marketing can become misleading. A 15,000x maximum does not tell you how likely the maximum result is, nor does it make the game more profitable to play.

It simply defines the highest payout permitted by the game’s mathematical model.

FeatureSpins and Bonus Buy Options

Information published ahead of release indicates that Buried By Night can offer enhanced FeatureSpin and Bonus Buy options in jurisdictions where these mechanics are permitted.

Reported versions include a Bonushunt mode that increases feature-trigger frequency, a Fast Hands mode focused on VS symbols, and direct access to selected free-spins rounds. Costs and RTP can differ depending on the chosen mode.

Availability is highly jurisdiction-dependent.

For example, Great Britain’s regulated online-slot market imposes specific design requirements and stake limits. The UK Gambling Commission currently limits online-slot stakes to £5 per game cycle for players aged 25 and over and £2 for adults aged 18–24. Those limits have been in effect since 2025.

The regulator has also taken action against feature buy-in mechanics and required operators it contacted over such functionality to remove them.

Players should therefore rely on the version offered by a properly licensed operator in their jurisdiction rather than assuming every feature advertised internationally will be available locally.

Mobile Gameplay

Buried By Night is designed as a modern browser-based slot and is reported to support desktop, tablet and smartphone play, including portrait-oriented mobile layouts.

That should suit the compact 5×4 format well.

The game does not rely on a huge grid or dozens of tiny symbols, so its important mechanics remain relatively easy to follow on smaller screens. Features such as horizontal DuelRows are also visually intuitive: when a VS symbol stretches across the grid, it is immediately obvious which area of the reels has changed.

Actual performance will naturally depend on the casino platform, browser, device and network connection used to access the game.

What Makes Buried By Night Different?

The Western setting is not what distinguishes Buried By Night.

Its strongest idea is turning Hacksaw’s duel concept sideways.

The horizontal structure changes how multiplier wilds interact with the 14 paylines and gives the game a recognisable mechanic without requiring an oversized grid or complicated collection system.

Shoot to Kill then builds on that concept through DuelSpins and Nudges, while Six Feet Under offers a completely different style of bonus sequence.

That creates a better sense of progression than a slot that simply repeats the same mechanic with slightly higher multipliers in every bonus.

The trade-off is complexity. A player seeing Buried By Night for the first time may need to read the paytable carefully before fully understanding when VS symbols expand, how DuelRows qualify and how multiplier combinations are calculated.

For an audience already familiar with Hacksaw Gaming, however, the learning curve should be fairly manageable.

Review Verdict

Buried By Night looks like a mechanically ambitious addition to Hacksaw Gaming’s Western catalogue.

The familiar gunslinger setting may initially make the slot appear derivative, but the gameplay does enough to separate it from a routine cowboy release. Its horizontal DuelRows are the standout mechanic, particularly when multiple rows and multipliers become involved in the same win.

Shoot to Kill appears to offer the deepest bonus experience thanks to the combination of DuelSpin progression, guaranteed VS symbols and Nudges. Six Feet Under also deserves attention because its 100-win structure is noticeably different from a conventional fixed free-spins round.

The other side of the equation is volatility.

Buried By Night is built around multipliers, enhanced feature states and a maximum potential of 15,000x. Players should expect the mathematical profile of a high-variance slot rather than smooth or predictable returns.

The RTP situation is also worth checking carefully. The best reported version offers 96.32%, but lower configurations exist, meaning the precise version offered by a casino can make a significant difference over the long term.

As of 2 October 2026, Buried By Night remains scheduled for release on 10 November 2026. This review therefore reflects the pre-release specifications currently available rather than claiming experience from a completed global casino launch. Once the game goes live, the final paytable, RTP and market-specific feature set should always take precedence over preview information.

For players interested in feature-heavy, multiplier-driven Hacksaw Gaming slots, Buried By Night is certainly one to watch. For players who dislike high volatility or prefer simple, frequent payouts, its mathematical style may be less appealing.
