The lead comes out, but your dog takes a little longer to get up. The stairs need more thought than they used to. On a favourite walk, the dog who normally sets the pace is suddenly happy to follow.

It would be easy to call it laziness, or simply another birthday catching up with them. But changes in how a dog moves deserve a closer look.

For owners considering how to support their pet’s joints, YuMove’s joint supplement range is one option to discuss with their vet. And there is a substantial saving for anyone starting a subscription: 50% off the first two months, followed by 20% off ongoing orders.

A slower start can be worth noticing

Dogs cannot explain that getting out of bed feels different today. Owners often have to spot the changes themselves.

Veterinary charity PDSA lists difficulty getting up, reluctance to jump or use stairs, and general slowing down among the signs of stiffness. It advises contacting a vet if a dog is limping or stiff, rather than assuming age is the explanation. PDSA’s guidance explains what to look for.

These changes can have different causes. A supplement is not a substitute for finding out what is wrong, and it does not replace prescribed treatment.

Once that conversation has happened, owners can explore whether daily joint support would be appropriate for their dog.

What is inside YuMove?

YuMove Joint Support for Adult Dogs is aimed at adult dogs beginning to show signs of joint stiffness.

The formula includes green-lipped mussel, a source of omega-3 fatty acids, alongside glucosamine and hyaluronic acid. YuMove describes it as designed to help ease stiffness, support joint structure and promote mobility.

The brand says its supplements are formulated by vets, backed by more than 15 years of scientific research and support two million dogs a year.

There are also different options within the YuMove joint-support range, including tablets and bites. Choosing means looking at the individual formula, the feeding instructions and your dog’s needs, rather than assuming every product will suit every pet.

That is particularly worth discussing with a vet if your dog already takes medication or has a diagnosed condition.

Half price to start, with a smaller discount afterwards

The introductory subscription offer cuts the cost by 50% for the first two months. After that, subscribers receive 20% off ongoing orders.

The amount paid depends on the product and quantity selected, so owners should check both the initial and continuing price. Deliveries and payments repeat automatically on the chosen schedule.

YuMove says there is no minimum commitment, and subscribers can skip, pause or cancel their subscription. Free UK delivery is advertised, with exclusions applying.

A six-week opportunity to assess the difference

There is another detail for owners weighing up whether to try something new: the six-week money-back guarantee.

YuMove says products purchased through its website qualify. After trying a product for six weeks from delivery, customers who have not seen a difference have a further 30 days to claim a refund under the guarantee’s terms.

That gives owners a defined period to assess the product, rather than expecting an overnight transformation.

For dogs whose vet considers a supplement suitable, the YuMove offer makes starting that routine less expensive.

But the first step costs nothing: pay attention to the small changes. If the stairs, the morning stretch or the usual walk seem harder, that is a conversation worth having.
