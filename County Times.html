RHAYADER and District Community Support has set up a fundraiser, after revealing last month that it faces “very difficult decisions” over its future.

Also known as The Arches, the community group announced at the end of September that its trustees are developing a financial recovery and delivery plan, which will include changes to charges and service hours, as it looks to remain a vital hub for Rhayader residents.

It said it is facing decisions that will affect its ability to deliver current level of services.  

“Following our recent announcement last week about the financial challenges we are currently facing, we wanted to share one of the ways you can help us during this difficult time,” The Arches said on Thursday, October 1, in a post on its Facebook page.

“We have set up a fundraiser to help support Rhayader & District Community Support (The Arches) and the vital services we provide across our community.

“Every donation, no matter how big or small, will help us as we work through this challenging period and look towards the future.

“The Arches is here for people in our community of all ages, and we know that many local people have personal connections to the services we provide – whether you, a family member, a friend or someone you know has received support from us.

“If you are able to donate, we would be incredibly grateful. If you're not in a position to donate, sharing our fundraiser can be just as valuable.

“The more people who know about the challenges we are facing, the more support we can hopefully reach.

“We know times are difficult for everyone, which is why we truly appreciate every bit of support.

“Together, we can help protect the services that so many people in our community rely on.”

The Arches operates a community transport and luncheon club scheme, and works with local organisations, groups and individuals to encourage community involvement, social interaction and independence.

“Like so many small charities across the country, Rhayader & District Community Support (The Arches) is having to deal with increasing operational costs,” the organisation said in its original post.

“We are facing very difficult decisions which will impact our ability to deliver our current level of services.  

“A financial recovery and delivery plan is currently being developed which will include changes to charges and service hours.

“The trustees, staff and volunteers at The Arches are committed to doing everything possible to keep our services running and minimise any disruption.

For more information about The Arches, or to get in touch and support it, visit the Facebook page, check them out on Instagram @thearchesrhayader, phone 01597 810921, email info@rdcs.org.uk, visit https://www.thearchesrhayader.co.uk/ or pop in to see them, on West Street.

You can also support the fundraiser by visiting https://shorturl.at/07Mhd.
