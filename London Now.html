An actor who starred in Peaky Blinders has admitted to speeding in one of the capital's 20mph zones.

Joe Cole, known for his role as John Shelby in the hit drama series, appeared in court documents in which he is alleged to have been behind the wheel of a BMW 1 series when it was seen doing 24mph near Wandsworth Common on May 28.

The 37-year-old, who also appeared in Black Mirror and Gangs Of London, pleaded guilty to the offence last week by the single justice procedure.

Admitting the offence, Cole, who lives in Kingston-upon-Thames, wrote: "I am an actor and have been abroad for the last few months.
