Soap Opera Spoilers This Week: What to Expect October 5-9

Soap spoilers for October 5-9 bring big secrets, relationship drama and major moves across Beyond the Gates, Days, B&B, GH and Y&R.

All Soap Spoilers October 5-9, 2026 bring plenty of drama across all five daytime soaps. From Beyond the Gates to Days of Our Lives, The Bold and the Beautiful, General Hospital, and The Young and the Restless, the week is packed with secrets, relationship trouble, bold moves, and a few surprises.

Get your full TV Guide on what to expect on the daytime soap operas below!

Key Takeaways

  • Beyond the Gates brings family drama, power plays, and shocking revelations.
  • Joy’s plans face new obstacles on Days of Our Lives.
  • Will and Katie clash over Dylan on The Bold and the Beautiful.
  • Sonny faces more pressure on General Hospital.
  • Nick’s secret and Lily’s health scare take center stage on The Young and the Restless.

Beyond the Gates Spoilers This Week

The week kicks off on Monday with Anita receiving troubling news from Vernon, while Dani gets iced out. Bill makes it clear he will not give up easily. Meanwhile, Nicole gives Deanna a reality check, and Vanessa dodges major questions. Martin and Smitty host a full house, Jacob and Naomi align their priorities, and Tyrell drops a major bombshell on his family.

Then on Tuesday, an update from Anita and Dani completely blindsides Nicole. Vernon puts Bill on notice, and Hayley scores a big win that comes at a high cost. Elsewhere, Leslie defends her recent actions, Jacob confronts Izaiah, Kat’s bold statements leave Eva shaken, and Chelsea issues a critical warning.

By Wednesday, Vernon steps in to save the day. Dani presses Bill for answers while Andre creates trouble for Ted. Meanwhile, Hayley gets caught off guard, and a distracted Izaiah leaves Eva facing the future alone. Additionally, Leslie stays devoted to stirring up drama, and Marcel and Joey work on their persuasion skills.

Moving into Thursday, Andre dredges up Dani’s past. Anita turns to music for comfort, while Nicole speaks candidly with Vanessa. Meanwhile, Ted and Ashley team up behind the scenes. Leslie’s actions send Hayley scrambling, Randy reaches his limit, and Noah offers Deanna a fresh perspective.

Closing out the week on Friday, Vernon tells Anita the hard truth. Nicole follows through on a promise, and Naomi stands her ground. After taking a huge risk, Madison leans on Chelsea for support. Meanwhile, Smitty sets strict boundaries with Martin, Vanessa and Joey hear Heather out, and Ted’s trip with Shanice becomes unforgettable.

Days of Our Lives Spoilers This Week

On Monday, the week kicks off with Joy starting to worry about what the future holds for her, while Alex catches Stephanie completely off guard with a surprise. Meanwhile, Chanel steps up to support Johnny as he struggles through a tough choice, and Kate gets a new idea to help Maggie out.

On Tuesday, Rachel gives Brady a genuine cause for concern, while Roman catches Kate by surprise. Elsewhere, Theo agrees to step up and lend Gabi a hand—and Philip hatches a brand-new plan.

On Wednesday, Sarah and Tate threaten Joy’s plans, while Jada and Shawn manage to share a romantic moment together. Leo’s good day takes a sudden bad turn, EJ confronts Rita regarding Dominic, and Brady is forced into making a really difficult call.

On Thursday, Dante surprises Sydney, while Paulina firmly refuses to give up on Lani. Chanel asks Johnny to make her a promise, Marlena drops a shocking confession on Belle, and Jada corners EJ with some tough questions.

The week closes on Friday with Joy being completely unhappy with Kayla’s discharge instructions, while Stephanie and Alex sneak away to steal a quiet, private moment. Elsewhere, Belle enlists Chad to help her out, Steve gets some troubling news from Johnny, and Kristen tracks down Sophia.

The Bold and the Beautiful Soap Spoilers This Week

On Monday, Will confides in Wyatt about what’s on his mind, while Daphne is left completely stunned when Carter drops a piece of surprising news on her.

On Tuesday, Daphne demands answers and questions why she was left in the dark for so long. Meanwhile, Electra is eager and ready to prove herself in a big way.

On Wednesday, Will draws a firm line in the sand with Katie. Over at Forrester, R.J. and Electra reassure Zende that he’s an integral part of their team moving forward.

On Thursday, Electra is furious after accidentally overhearing a private conversation involving Dylan. Elsewhere, Eric and Ridge prepare to put their full backing behind the new Electra line.

The week closes on Friday with Zende being put right on the spot. Meanwhile, Katie and Hope are feeling genuinely optimistic about Logan’s next big power move.

General Hospital Spoilers This Week

On Monday, Sonny steps up and challenges Justine, while Josslyn refuses to wait around and takes matters into her own hands. Meanwhile, Britt and Willow share a deep chat about matters of the heart, Jason gets taken to task, and Serena suddenly finds herself stuck in a bind.

On Tuesday, Felicia drops by with some very welcome news for Anna, while Brook Lynn opens up and confides in Valentin. Elsewhere, Hudson tries his best to talk a heated Josslyn down, Lulu makes a daring play, and Willow and Chase discuss the state of his marriage.

On Wednesday, Carly turns to Anna to ask for a major favor, while Tracy surprisingly bites her tongue during an exchange with Gio. Drew and Alexis get into a heated clash, young Scout tries to broker peace between Danny and Rocco, and Lulu keeps pushing forward to pursue a new lead.

On Thursday, Carly steps in to offer Sonny some much-needed support, while Lulu’s latest published article starts making huge waves across Port Charles. Ava pitches an intriguing new target to Drew, Curtis gets a total shock, and Chase officially throws down the gauntlet.

The week closes on Friday with Britt offering a critical lifeline to someone in need, while Anna steps up to lend her professional expertise. Sonny reassures Gio that everything will be okay, Portia makes her loyalties crystal clear, and Dante’s ongoing investigation hits a sudden roadblock.

The Young and the Restless Soap Spoilers This Week

On Monday, Victor plays his cards extremely close to the vest to keep Nick protected, while Lily hits a major snag when an unexpected health concern throws her world into uncertainty. Meanwhile, Phyllis is riding high after successfully sealing the deal.

On Tuesday, Victor follows through and makes good on a promise to Phyllis. Nikki puts Nick through an intense interrogation about his recent choices, and Sharon finally turns the page to make a fresh start.

On Wednesday, former rivals Phyllis and Sharon form an unexpected pact, while Matt Clark gets a severe rude awakening. Audra decides she’s completely done playing games and lays down an ultimatum for both Adam and Billy.

On Thursday, Billy finds himself tossed straight into the hot seat. Meanwhile, as the drinks flow for Sally’s bachelorette party, a surprise guest crashes the celebration.

The week closes on Friday with Cane pushing a reluctant Lily to finally come clean about what she’s dealing with. Audra and Chelsea are forced to call an uneasy truce and set their differences aside, while Billy’s relationship with Sally faces its biggest test yet.
